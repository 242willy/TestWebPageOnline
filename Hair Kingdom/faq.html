01 / The details

Hair quality & products

QIs your hair 100% human hair?

AYes! Our hair is 100% human hair, offering a natural look and feel. With proper care, your bundles can maintain their softness, shine, and quality.

QCan I dye or bleach the hair?

AYes, our human hair can be colored and customized. However, we recommend having a professional stylist handle chemical processing to help maintain the hair's quality.

QCan the hair be curled and straightened?

AAbsolutely! Our bundles can be curled, straightened, and styled just like your natural hair. We recommend using a heat protectant before applying heat.

QDoes the hair shed or tangle?

AMinimal shedding and tangling may occur with normal use. Proper washing, conditioning, detangling, and storage will help maintain the hair's longevity.

QHow long does the hair last?

AWith proper maintenance and care, our bundles can last for an extended period. Longevity depends on how frequently the hair is styled and maintained.
