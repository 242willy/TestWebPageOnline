A little care goes a long way

Keep your crown
looking like royalty.

Our 100% human hair bundles can last 4+ years with proper maintenance. Since the hair no longer receives natural oils from a scalp, keeping it clean, hydrated, and protected is up to you.

01♧

Wash & condition

Use sulfate- and alcohol-free shampoo and conditioner. Smooth shampoo downward from weft to tips in lukewarm water—never rub, bunch, or massage. Deep condition weekly for 15–30 minutes, then rinse with cool water.

02⌁

Dry & detangle

Skip the blow dryer. Gently pat with a microfiber towel and lay flat or hang to air dry. Never brush while soaking wet. Once dry or damp, detangle from the tips upward with a wide-tooth comb or flexible brush.

03✧

Style with care

Always use a lightweight heat protectant and keep hot tools under 350°F (175°C). Hair can be bleached or dyed, but chemical processing can dry it out—visit a professional colorist if you're not a licensed stylist.

04☾

Protect it at night

Never sleep on wet hair. Before bed, brush gently and secure in a loose ponytail, braid, or wrap under a silk bonnet. A silk or satin pillowcase helps reduce friction and moisture loss from cotton.

Our return &
refund policy.

We take immense pride in our premium human hair bundles. Since extensions are a hygienic product, we follow strict return standards to protect everyone's health and safety.

↺7-day window
From the date your tracking shows delivery
01

Return & exchange window

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 7 days of the delivery date shown by your tracking. After 7 days, we’re unable to offer a refund or exchange.

02

Strict return conditions

Bundles must be in the exact condition in which they arrived. For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept bundles if security ties or rubber bands have been cut, untied, or loosened; hair has been washed, co-washed, combed, brushed, or fingers run through it; hair has been installed, cut, bleached, colored, or treated with styling products; or original packaging, silk tags, or labels are missing. Any alteration to the bundle structure makes it ineligible for return.

03

How to start a return

Within 7 days of delivery, contact our support team using the email address listed in your order confirmation. Include your order number and photos showing the hair is still securely tied and untampered with. Once approved, we’ll send the correct return address. Customers pay return shipping; original shipping costs are non-refundable.

04

Refunds & processing

When we receive and inspect your return, we’ll email you to confirm receipt and let you know whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are automatically credited to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. If returned hair is altered or used, the refund will be denied and the item will be shipped back at your expense.

05

Order cancellations & changes

You may request an order change or cancellation within 2 hours of placing your order. Once an order has been packed and handed to the shipping carrier, we can no longer cancel or modify it.
